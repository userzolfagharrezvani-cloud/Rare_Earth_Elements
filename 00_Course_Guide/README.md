## عناصر خاکی کمیاب: شیمی، استخراج، جداسازی و بازیافت

**Level / سطح:** MSc–PhD / کارشناسی ارشد و دکتری  
**Fields / رشته‌ها:** Inorganic Chemistry, Materials Engineering, Extractive Metallurgy / شیمی معدنی، مهندسی مواد، متالورژی استخراجی

## Learning outcomes | اهداف یادگیری
By the end of this course, students should be able to:
1. Explain lanthanide electron configurations, oxidation states, lanthanide contraction, coordination chemistry, and selected optical/magnetic properties.
2. Distinguish major REE-bearing minerals and evaluate ore composition and liberation.
3. Compare physical beneficiation methods using grade, recovery, and mass-balance calculations.
4. Explain pretreatment and leaching chemistry, including the effects of pH, temperature, reagent concentration, and mineralogy.
5. Compare precipitation, solvent extraction, ion exchange, adsorption, membranes, and electrochemical approaches.
6. Assess primary and secondary feedstocks and discuss recycling routes.
7. Critically evaluate process selectivity, reagent and energy use, waste streams, and environmental trade-offs.
8. Find, verify, and cite peer-reviewed scientific literature using DOI and publisher pages.

## Quantitative toolkit | ابزار محاسباتی
- Grade (mass fraction) and enrichment ratio / عیار و نسبت پرعیارسازی
- Recovery: (R(\%)=100\times\frac{m_{product}x_{product}}{m_{feed}x_{feed}}\)
- Distribution ratio: (D=C_{org}/C_{aq}\) under explicitly stated conditions
- Separation factor: \(\alpha_{A/B}=D_A/D_B\)
- Leaching efficiency from feed and residue assays
- Mass balance around each unit operation and the complete flowsheet

Definitions and equations must state the basis, units, and assumptions. Do not compare recoveries reported under different feed compositions or analytical bases without qualification.

## Safety and scope | ایمنی و دامنه
REE processing may involve corrosive reagents, high temperatures, fine particulates, and naturally occurring thorium or uranium in some ores and residues. Procedures require suitable facilities, risk assessment, waste management, and regulatory compliance. This course guide is conceptual and does not replace local safety procedures.
