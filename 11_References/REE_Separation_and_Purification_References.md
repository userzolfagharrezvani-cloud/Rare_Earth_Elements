# REE Separation and Purification — Verified Starting Bibliography
# جداسازی و خالص‌سازی REE — کتاب‌نامه آغازین

## Reviews and research papers | مقالات مروری و پژوهشی

1. Chen, Z. et al. “Recent advances in selective separation technologies of rare earth elements: a review.” *Journal of Environmental Chemical Engineering* 10 (2022), 107104. Elsevier.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jece.2021.107104  
   Focus: comparison of selective separation technologies including precipitation, ion exchange, solvent extraction, membranes and adsorption.

2. Do-Thanh, C.-L.; Luo, H.; Dai, S. “A perspective on task-specific ionic liquids for the separation of rare earth elements.” *RSC Sustainability* 1 (2023), 1168–1176. Royal Society of Chemistry.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/D3SU00007A  
   Publisher page: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2023/su/d3su00007a  
   Focus: ionic-liquid systems for REE liquid–liquid extraction and separation.

3. Opare, E. O.; Struhs, E.; Mirkouei, A. “A comparative state-of-technology review and future directions for rare earth element separation.” *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews* 143 (2021), 110917. Elsevier.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2021.110917  
   Focus: technology landscape, maturity and future directions.

4. “Antagonistic Role of Aqueous Complexation in the Solvent Extraction and Separation of Rare Earth Ions.” *ACS Central Science* (2021). American Chemical Society.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acscentsci.1c00960  
   Focus: molecular mechanism and the role of aqueous complexation in REE separation.

5. Kumar, A.; Geng, H.; Schelter, E. J. “Harnessing magnetic fields for rare-earth complex crystallization–separations in aqueous solutions.” *RSC Advances* 12 (2022), 27895–27898. Royal Society of Chemistry.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/D2RA04729B  
   Focus: experimental study of magnetic-field-assisted crystallization separation; this is a research paper, not a review.

6. Merroune, A. et al. “A comprehensive review on solvent extraction technologies of rare earth elements from different acidic media: Current challenges and future perspectives.” *Journal of Industrial and Engineering Chemistry* (2024). Elsevier.  
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jiec.2024.04.042  
   Focus: solvent extraction from different acidic media and process challenges.

## How to use the sources | روش استفاده از منابع
- Use reviews to map methods and identify primary studies.
- For numerical values, extractant recipes or performance claims, consult the original experimental paper and its supporting information.
- Confirm the full author list, journal volume, pages/article number, year and DOI on the publisher site before adding a formal citation.
- Do not infer industrial viability from a single-ion test or a high distribution ratio alone.
- Check figure reuse licenses; original diagrams are preferred for teaching materials.

برای مرور روش‌ها از مقالات مروری و برای اعداد و شرایط عملیاتی از مقاله پژوهشی اصلی استفاده کنید. اطلاعات کتاب‌شناختی را در صفحه ناشر کنترل کنید و کارایی صنعتی را از یک آزمون تک‌یونی یا ضریب توزیع بالا به‌تنهایی نتیجه نگیرید.
