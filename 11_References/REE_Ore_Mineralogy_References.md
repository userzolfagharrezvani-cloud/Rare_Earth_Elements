# REE Ore Mineralogy — Topic Bibliography
# کتاب‌نامه موضوعی کانی‌شناسی و منابع زمین‌شناختی REE

The following records were selected for this lesson. DOI and publisher metadata should be rechecked before formal publication or quotation.

## Open-access reviews and papers | مقالات مروری و دسترسی آزاد

1. Chong, S.; Riley, B. J.; Lu, X.; Du, J.; Mahadevan, T.; Hegde, V. **“Synthesis and properties of anhydrous rare-earth phosphates, monazite and xenotime: a review.”** *RSC Advances* 14 (2024), 18978–19000.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1039/D4RA01142B
   - RSC publisher page: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2024/ra/d4ra01142b
   - Topics: monazite/xenotime crystal structures, composition, substitutions, durability, and actinide-bearing natural minerals.
   - License: publisher page identifies this as open access under a Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence; check the current page for exact reuse requirements.

2. Russo, S. C.; González-Álvarez, I.; Cocker, H. A.; McCoy-West, A. J. **“The fundamentals of rare earth element ion adsorption clay deposits: A mineral systems approach for exploration.”** *Journal of Geochemical Exploration* 278 (2025), article 107845.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gexplo.2025.107845
   - Publisher page: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gexplo.2025.107845
   - Topics: sources, mobilization, adsorption, preservation, weathering profiles, and controls on LREE/HREE enrichment.

3. **“Review of rare earth element (REE) adsorption on and desorption from clay minerals: Application to formation and mining of ion-adsorption REE deposits.”** *Ore Geology Reviews* 157 (2023), article 105446.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oregeorev.2023.105446
   - Publisher page: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oregeorev.2023.105446
   - Topics: clay mineral evolution, REE adsorption/desorption, deposit genesis, and leaching controls.

4. Jung, M.; Yoo, K. **“Flotation Researches of Bastnaesite, Monazite, Xenotime: A Review.”** *Journal of the Korean Society of Mineral and Energy Resources Engineers* 57 (2020), 652–670.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.32390/ksmer.2020.57.6.652
   - Publisher page: https://www.jksmer.or.kr/articles/xml/WDkJ/
   - Topics: mineral chemistry, surface characteristics, collector adsorption, and flotation reagent schemes.

5. **“Processing the ores of rare-earth elements.”** *MRS Bulletin* (2022), Springer Nature.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1557/s43577-022-00288-4
   - Publisher page: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-022-00288-4
   - Topics: ore processing, beneficiation, and separation challenges.

## Suggested reading strategy | روش مطالعه پیشنهادی
- Start with the RSC review for monazite/xenotime crystal chemistry.
- Read the 2025 *Journal of Geochemical Exploration* review for ion-adsorption clay systems.
- Use the 2023 *Ore Geology Reviews* paper for adsorption/desorption mechanisms.
- Use the flotation review and *MRS Bulletin* article to connect mineral properties with beneficiation.
- Verify full metadata and license details on the publisher page before citing or reusing figures.

## Reference quality checklist | چک‌لیست کنترل کیفیت
