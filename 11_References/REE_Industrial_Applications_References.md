# REE Industrial Applications — Reading List
# کاربردهای صنعتی REE — فهرست مطالعه

## Foundational sources | منابع پایه

1. Goodenough, K. M. et al. “The Rare Earth Elements: Demand, Global Resources, and Challenges for Resourcing Future Generations.” *Natural Resources Research* 27 (2018), 201–216. Springer. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11053-017-9336-5  
   Useful for resources, demand and supply context; not a current market forecast.

2. Binnemans, K. et al. “Recycling of rare earths: a critical review.” *Journal of Cleaner Production* 51 (2013), 1–22. Elsevier. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2012.12.037  
   Useful for recycling routes and end-of-life considerations.

3. Rademaker, J. H.; Kleijn, R.; Yang, Y. “Recycling as a strategy against rare earth element criticality: a systemic evaluation of the potential yield of NdFeB magnet recycling.” *Environmental Science & Technology* 47 (2013), 10129–10136. ACS. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/es305007w  
   Useful for systemic magnet-recycling analysis.

4. *Handbook on the Physics and Chemistry of Rare Earths*. Elsevier series.  
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/bookseries/handbook-on-the-physics-and-chemistry-of-rare-earths  
   Scholarly reference series for detailed physical chemistry and materials properties.

5. Royal Society of Chemistry periodic table: https://www.rsc.org/periodic-table

## Update the application-specific bibliography | تکمیل منابع تخصصی هر کاربرد
For a publication-ready teaching package, add recent peer-reviewed primary papers for:
- Nd–Fe–B grain-boundary diffusion, coercivity and reduced Dy/Tb usage;
- Eu³⁺/Tb³⁺ phosphors, energy transfer and thermal quenching;
- Nd:YAG, Er³⁺ telecom materials and rare-earth-doped glass;
- CeO₂ redox chemistry, oxygen vacancies and catalytic performance;
- La-containing optical glass and rare-earth-stabilized ceramics.

برای بسته آموزشی آماده انتشار، مقالات پژوهشی داوری‌شده جدید درباره مهندسی مرزدانه آهنربا، فسفرهای Eu/Tb، لیزرهای آلاییده با REE، شیمی اکسایش–کاهش CeO₂، شیشه نوری حاوی La و سرامیک‌های پایدارشده با REE اضافه شود.

## Citation verification | راستی‌آزمایی استناد
Verify complete bibliographic metadata and DOI at the publisher before formal citation. Foundational sources should not be represented as recent publications. For quantitative claims, cite the primary experimental paper and report the test conditions and uncertainty.
