## Required journal-literature update | به‌روزرسانی ضروری مقالات
For each technique, the final teaching bibliography should include recent peer-reviewed method papers using REE-relevant matrices:
- ICP-MS/OES: digestion validation, spectral/isobaric interferences and certified reference materials.
- XRF: matrix correction and calibration for ores/concentrates.
- XRD: phase identification and quantitative Rietveld analysis of mixed rare-earth compounds.
- XPS/XANES: oxidation-state analysis, multiplet effects and surface/bulk differences.
- Raman/PL: rare-earth-doped materials, defect interpretation and optical quantification.
- SEM/TEM–EDS/EELS: nanoscale mapping and quantification limits.

برای هر روش، مقالات داوری‌شده جدیدی با ماتریس مرتبط با REE اضافه شود؛ به‌ویژه اعتبارسنجی هضم و تداخل در ICP، کالیبراسیون XRF، پالایش ریتولد، تحلیل حالت اکسایش، تفسیر رامان/فوتولومینسانس و محدودیت کمّی‌سازی EDS/EELS.

## Citation integrity | صحت استناد
This file deliberately separates technical databases and standards from journal literature. Before citing a journal paper, verify title, author list, journal, year, volume, pages/article number and DOI at the publisher. Do not label a standards page or database as a peer-reviewed paper. Report detection limits and uncertainty for the actual matrix and instrument configuration.
