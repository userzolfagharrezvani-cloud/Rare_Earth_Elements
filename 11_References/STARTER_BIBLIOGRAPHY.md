# Verified Starter Bibliography | کتاب‌نامه آغازینِ بررسی‌شده

This file is a starter list, not an exhaustive bibliography. Links below point to DOI/publisher records. Check article metadata and access conditions before citing a paper in a formal manuscript.

این فهرست برای شروع است و جامع نیست. پیوندها به رکورد DOI یا ناشر هدایت می‌شوند. پیش از استناد رسمی، فراداده مقاله و شرایط دسترسی را بررسی کنید.

## Ore processing and mineralogy | فرآوری کانسنگ و کانی‌شناسی

1. **“Processing the ores of rare-earth elements.”** *MRS Bulletin*, Springer Nature. Review of ore processing, separation routes, and efficiency challenges.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1557/s43577-022-00288-4
   - Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-022-00288-4

## Separation and purification | جداسازی و خالص‌سازی

2. **“The recent progress of ion exchange for the separation of rare earths from secondary resources – A review.”** *Hydrometallurgy* (2023).
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.hydromet.2023.106047
   - Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304386X23000294
   - Topics: ion exchange, secondary resources, sulfate media, selectivity, and process limitations.

3. **“Extraction and separation of heavy rare earth elements: A review.”** *Separation and Purification Technology* (2021).
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.seppur.2021.119263
   - Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1383586621009734
   - Topics: heavy REE resources, leaching, solvent extraction, solid–liquid separation, and purification.

## Recycling and secondary resources | بازیافت و منابع ثانویه

4. Pimassoni, Y. S., Lima, M. T. W. D. C., Yamane, L. H., and Siman, R. R. **“The recovery of rare earth elements from waste electrical and electronic equipment: A review.”** *Hydrometallurgy* 222 (2023), article 106156.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.hydromet.2023.106156
   - Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304386X23001314
   - Topics: fluorescent lamps, magnets, NiMH batteries, circuit boards, LEDs, and scale-up challenges.

5. Erust, C., Karacahan, M. K., and Uysal, T. **“Hydrometallurgical Roadmaps and Future Strategies for Recovery of Rare Earth Elements.”** *Mineral Processing and Extractive Metallurgy Review* (2022).
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/08827508.2022.2073591
   - Publisher: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/08827508.2022.2073591
   - Topics: primary and secondary resources, roasting, leaching, precipitation, solvent extraction, and ion exchange.

## Bibliography quality checklist | چک‌لیست کیفیت منابع
- [ ] Exact title matches publisher record.
- [ ] Author list and year checked.
- [ ] DOI resolves and matches the same work.
- [ ] Journal, volume, pages/article number recorded where available.
- [ ] Review vs. original research identified correctly.
- [ ] Access/license status checked before sharing full text or figures.
- [ ] Claims in the lesson are linked to the relevant source, not just a general reading list.

## Publisher coverage to expand | ناشران برای تکمیل
Future searches should include relevant papers from Elsevier, Royal Society of Chemistry, ACS Publications, Springer Nature, Taylor & Francis, and other reputable peer-reviewed sources. Publisher names alone do not guarantee quality; each individual record must be checked.
