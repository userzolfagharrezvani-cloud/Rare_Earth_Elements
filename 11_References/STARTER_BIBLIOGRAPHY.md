### Verification checklist | چک‌لیست
- Exact title and authors match publisher metadata.
- DOI resolves to the same work.
- Year, journal, volume and article number/pages checked.
- Review vs. primary research classified correctly.
- Figure license and access conditions checked.
- Each lesson claim cites the specific source that supports it.

Further topic-specific searches should cover Elsevier, Royal Society of Chemistry, ACS Publications, Springer Nature and Taylor & Francis. Publisher reputation alone does not guarantee that a particular reference is accurate.
