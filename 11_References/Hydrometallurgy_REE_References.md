# Hydrometallurgical Extraction of REEs — Reading List
# فهرست منابع استخراج هیدرومتالورژیکی REE

## Selected review literature | منابع مروری منتخب

1. **“Hydrometallurgical Roadmaps and Future Strategies for Recovery of Rare Earth Elements.”** *Mineral Processing and Extractive Metallurgy Review* (2022).
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/08827508.2022.2073591
   - Publisher: Taylor & Francis.
   - Use: broad overview of hydrometallurgical recovery strategies; consult the publisher page for full author and volume metadata before formal citation.

2. **“Processing the ores of rare-earth elements.”** *MRS Bulletin* (2022).
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1557/s43577-022-00288-4
   - Publisher: Springer Nature.
   - Use: connect ore mineralogy, beneficiation, and downstream processing.

3. Russo, S. C. et al. **“The fundamentals of rare earth element ion adsorption clay deposits: A mineral systems approach for exploration.”** *Journal of Geochemical Exploration* 278 (2025), 107845.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gexplo.2025.107845
   - Publisher: Elsevier.
   - Use: geology and controls of ion-adsorption clay deposits; not a substitute for process-specific leaching tests.

4. **“Review of rare earth element (REE) adsorption on and desorption from clay minerals: Application to formation and mining of ion-adsorption REE deposits.”** *Ore Geology Reviews* 157 (2023), 105446.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oregeorev.2023.105446
   - Publisher: Elsevier.
   - Use: adsorption/desorption behavior and factors relevant to ion-adsorption deposits.

## Citation verification | راستی‌آزمایی استناد
This reading list is a starting point, not a substitute for checking publisher metadata. Before using a source in a thesis or article, verify exact title, authors, journal, year, volume/pages or article number, DOI, article type, and access/license status on the publisher site.
